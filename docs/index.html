About

Performance engineering leadership for complex digital transformation.

I am a performance engineering lead with 20+ years across banking and healthcare, including TD Bank, Kaiser Permanente, Bank of America, and Citibank. My work spans workload modelling, NFR/SLO definition, bottleneck analysis, capacity right-sizing, and confidence-level reporting to executives on release readiness.

My experience covers web, API, microservice, database, and core banking platforms on Azure and AKS, with hands-on expertise in JMeter, LoadRunner, Dynatrace (11 years), Splunk, Datadog, Grafana, Jenkins, and GitHub Actions, plus JavaScript/Node.js tooling, Selenium- and Puppeteer-based UI testing, and AI-assisted engineering with GitHub Copilot and Ollama.

I have managed performance engineering teams of up to 30 engineers in India while leading delivery with teams in the US and Canada. Today I work hands-on as an individual contributor, so clients get both practice leadership and direct engineering execution.
